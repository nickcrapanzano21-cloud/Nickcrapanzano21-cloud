## Professional Background

My career combines creative audio production with systems-level engineering.

I have produced music professionally since 2003, working across rock, metal, folk, world, singer/songwriter, and commercial audio projects. My production experience includes arrangement, performance direction, vocal production, recording, editing, mixing, and preparation for mastering.

In broadcast engineering, I have supported nationally syndicated spoken-word programming, engineered more than 1,000 live remote broadcasts, designed and built production environments, trained technical staff, and diagnosed complex analog, digital, networked, and software-based audio systems.

## Audio Engineering Approach

I approach technical problems from source to destination, testing each stage of the signal path until the fault is isolated.

In creative production, I work in the opposite direction: I begin by understanding the intended outcome, then translate artistic language and performance goals into recording, microphone, signal-chain, processing, and workflow decisions.

## Current Focus

I am exploring the intersection of traditional audio engineering, human voice production, and emerging AI-driven audio technologies.

I am particularly interested in how new voice technologies can preserve the qualities that make performances believable: timbre, clarity, dynamics, emotional intent, and performance nuance.
